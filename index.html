01 / WHY NOW

Local roots.
Real experience.

My grandparents raised me in Gardner with a simple idea: take care of the place that takes care of you.

I spent five terms representing Ward 3 on the Gardner City Council. That meant budgets and public meetings, but also neighborhood concerns, basic services, and the follow-through people should be able to expect from their city.

Since then, I've worked on the executive side of municipal government—managing budgets, projects, grants, procurement, and the day-to-day reality of making public services work. It has given me a different view of the same issues residents see every day.

Before deciding whether to seek another public office, I want to understand what Gardner needs next and whether I can make a meaningful contribution.

01

Taxes & the budget

What are the real trade-offs? What can the city afford, and can residents follow the money?

02

Roads & infrastructure

Are we maintaining what we have and planning projects before repairs become emergencies?

03

Public safety

Do police, fire, EMS, and dispatch have the staff, equipment, and coordination to serve well?

04

Housing & neighborhoods

How can families and seniors find attainable homes while keeping development connected to infrastructure capacity?
