A NOTE FROM NATE

It's good to think about Gardner again.

I grew up here. My grandparents raised me to take care of the place that takes care of you.

That's why I got involved in city government in the first place. I represented Ward 3 from 2013 to 2022 and learned as much talking to residents as I ever did sitting in a council meeting.

I've been on the other side of the table since then. Running a town gives you a healthy respect for what a budget really says, what a department needs, and what happens after a vote is taken.

So I'm looking at Gardner again. No big announcement and no decision on an office. Just a serious look at whether the city could use what I've learned.
